# Words-Wizard 🧙‍♂️✨

**A magical word-learning adventure for young heroes (ages 7–12)**

Turn vocabulary practice into an epic journey. In Words-Wizard, children don't just memorize words — they use them as spells, keys, and weapons to explore enchanted worlds, defeat monsters, and raise their very own magical pet.

---

## 🌟 What Makes Words-Wizard Special

- **Learn by playing, not by drilling** — every word is a tool to unlock the next part of the story.
- **Choose your own adventure** — pick any of 10 story worlds, any word bank, at any time.
- **Grow a magical pet** — the more you learn, the more your pet evolves.
- **Safe and balanced** — built-in healthy-use limits keep screen time fun, not endless.
- **Parents stay in the loop** — gentle progress reports without the nagging.

## 🎮 Five Ways to Play

Children learn best when different senses are engaged. Words-Wizard offers five game styles, each training a different skill.

| Game | Skill | How It Works |
|---|---|---|
| **Spelling Challenge** | Spelling | Tap letters on a magical keyboard to spell the word. |
| **Match & Clear** | Word meaning | Pair English words with their Chinese meanings to clear the board. |
| **Word Bubbles** | Listening | Hear a word, then pop the correct floating bubble. |
| **RPG Word Maze** | Everything | The main adventure — use words to open doors, defeat monsters, and solve puzzles. |
| **Dictation Mode** | Spelling + listening | Listen and type the word. Auto-graded instantly. |

## 📚 Word Banks for Every Level

Children can pick the word bank that matches their grade — or challenge themselves with something harder.

**Built-in banks:**
- Primary School
- Junior High
- Senior High
- CET-4
- CET-6

**Make your own:**
- Teachers and parents can upload custom word lists (CSV, Excel, or JSON).
- Paste a word list directly, or add words one by one.
- Words are automatically checked for duplicates and safety.
- Share custom word banks with a class or group.

Each word bank comes with:
- The word itself
- Chinese meaning
- Pronunciation (audio)
- Example sentence
- Part of speech and difficulty tag

The app automatically adjusts question difficulty based on the chosen word bank — younger learners get more picture-based and listening questions, while older learners get context and synonym challenges.

## 🛡️ Healthy Play, Built In

Words-Wizard is designed so children can enjoy it without losing track of time.

**How it works:**
- **Magic Energy Bar** — instead of a scary countdown, children see their energy gently fade as they play. When it runs out, the pet says, *"The moon is up — time to rest, little hero."*
- **Age-appropriate limits:**
  - Ages 8–12: 30 minutes a day, 15 minutes per session
  - Ages 12–16: 45 minutes a day, 20 minutes per session
  - Under 8: parent-managed only
- **Available hours** — play is only open during reasonable daytime hours.
- **Natural stopping points** — the game saves automatically and shows a nightly summary, so children end on a positive note.

**For parents:**
- Set daily time limits and allowed hours
- Lock the current word bank (so kids can't switch to easier ones to "farm time")
- Get a gentle notification when the daily limit is reached
- Approve extra time requests on weekends or special occasions

**Teaching self-control:**
- **Time Bank** — complete learning tasks to earn time coins, then trade them for extra play minutes.
- **Self-Discipline Medal** — 7 days in a row of stopping on time (without being forced) earns a special badge and a limited pet outfit.
- **Weekly Report for Kids** — see how many times you managed your own time well. Celebrate the wins!

## 🚀 How to Get Started

1. **Open Words-Wizard** in any modern browser on a phone or tablet.
