Words Wizard — Privacy

Words Wizard is a children's vocabulary app. This page explains what the app stores, where it lives, and how deletion works. We collect only what is needed for a child's progress to follow their sign-in to another device.

What we store

  • Your Microsoft Entra object id (oid), so your progress follows you to another device. We do not store your email address, display name, or photo.
  • Your chosen buddy name and avatar, your topic, locale, XP, streak, Care Token state, and practice progress.
  • Session history and the counters shown in Settings, such as sessions completed and words learned or mastered.
  • A small set of device flags in your browser, such as whether the app is installed and whether read-aloud is available. These are not sent to the API.

Where it lives

  • Cloudflare D1 stores the oid and the per-user rows. Cloudflare also serves the app and the API.
  • Microsoft handles sign-in. The app never sees your password.
  • Your browser keeps the Entra session, any unsent practice, and the device flags. Signing out clears all three.

What we do not do

  • We do not read your email, contacts, or files.
  • We do not run analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not keep a copy after a verified deletion request.

Signing out

Signing out removes this browser's session, any unsent practice, and the device flags. Your saved progress stays with your account and returns when you sign in again.

Deletion

There is no delete button in the app. A parent or account holder can ask for deletion through the repository issue tracker. The operator confirms the request, deletes the account's row in Cloudflare D1, and checks that no related rows remain. This removes the profile, progress, sessions, and counters; the learning content is not personal data.

单词魔法师 — 隐私

单词魔法师是一款儿童英语单词应用。这里说明我们会保存什么、保存在哪里，以及如何删除。我们只收集让学习进度跟随登录账号到另一台设备所必需的信息。

我们保存的内容

  • Microsoft Entra 对象 ID（oid），用于在不同设备间同步学习进度。我们不会保存你的电子邮件地址、显示名称或照片。
  • 你选择的伙伴名字和头像、当前主题、语言、经验值、连续练习天数、关怀令牌状态，以及练习进度。
  • 练习记录，以及设置页显示的计数器，例如完成的练习次数、学会和掌握的单词数。
  • 浏览器中的少量设备标记，例如是否已安装应用、是否支持朗读。这些不会发送到 API。

保存在哪里

  • Cloudflare D1 保存 oid 和每位用户的记录。Cloudflare 也提供应用和 API。
  • Microsoft 负责登录。应用不会看到你的密码。
  • 浏览器保存 Entra 登录状态、尚未发送的练习和设备标记。退出登录会清除这三项。

我们不会做的事

  • 不会读取你的邮件、联系人或文件。
  • 不做统计分析、广告或第三方追踪。
  • 不会出售或分享你的数据。
  • 在确认删除请求后，不会保留副本。

退出登录

退出登录会清除这个浏览器里的登录状态、尚未发送的练习和设备标记。已保存的进度会留在你的账户中，再次登录即可继续。

删除

应用里没有删除按钮。家长或账户持有人可以通过仓库的问题页面提出删除请求。运维人员会确认请求，删除 Cloudflare D1 中该账户的记录，并检查没有相关记录残留。这会删除个人档案、进度、练习记录和计数器；学习内容本身不是个人数据。

联系方式： https://github.com/RhaegarC/words-wizard/issues
